# WL Helper Nav: notice

WL Helper Nav (`WLHelper_Nav`, `WLHelper_Nav_EK`, `WLHelper_Nav_Kalimdor`) is a
**modified copy, made on 2026-11-15 for WL Helper**, of **Polestar Nav 0.4.0**
(walking maps of 2026-11-14; first copied from 0.3.7.2, maps of 2026-11-11,
updated on 2026-11-16: see "Upstream updates" below), so WL Helper's GPS can walk real paths (round
mountains, through gates, down lifts) for players who do not have Polestar.

## What was changed

1. **Renamed globals**, so WL Helper Nav and Polestar Nav load together without
   clashing: `PolestarPathData` -> `WLHelperNavData`, `Polestar_NavEngine` ->
   `WLHelperNav`, `Polestar_NavCosts` -> `WLHelperNavCosts`,
   `PolestarNavChokepoints` -> `WLHelperNavChokepoints`; the part addons
   `Polestar_Nav_EK` -> `WLHelper_Nav_EK`, `Polestar_Nav_Kalimdor` ->
   `WLHelper_Nav_Kalimdor` (the maps' `part` field and comments).
2. **No Polestar dependency**: the router never read anything from the Polestar
   addon (only its `.toc` depended on it); the `.toc` files now depend on
   `WLHelper` (engine) and `WLHelper_Nav` (continent maps), and all three are
   grouped under WL Helper in the AddOn list (`## Group: WLHelper`). Polestar's
   journey planner, UI and arrow (`Nav/Walk.lua`, `Nav/Travel.lua`, ...) are
   not copied: `WalkTo` prices a walk as Polestar's `Nav/Walk.lua` does
   (faction, "balanced" style, home hub, water walking for a ghost, zone
   danger at the player's level).
3. **Packaging**: new `.toc` files (Interface 16001, LoadOnDemand), one line
   added as line 2 of every Lua file saying it is a modified copy, a comment
   in `Nav/Nav.xml`, `PACK.json` re-pinned (new paths and sha256 of the renamed
   files, a `copy` entry). In the generated files that carried no copyright
   (`Nav/Chokepoints.lua`, `Nav/Nav2991_1.lua`, `Nav/Nav2991_2.lua` and the
   continent parts) that line also names the Polestar authors and the licence. The packed map strings, the algorithm and the costs
   are byte-for-byte Polestar's: routes and costs are identical.
4. **Additions** at the end of `API.lua` (WL Helper's code, GPL-3.0-or-later):
   `PlayerPosition`, `MapToWorld`, `WorldToMap`, `WalkTo`, and the API
   documentation at its top. Polestar Nav's own notes in `API.lua` are kept,
   under a line saying so.
5. **Zone danger** (`Danger.lua`, new): adapted from the **Polestar** addon's
   `Nav/Danger.lua` (its `Mult`/`Table` rule: a zone 4+ levels above the
   character costs 3x, 10+ above 20x, the walk's start and goal zones left
   out; idea and threshold from Ding! by Yendi) and the zone levels of its
   `Maps/ZoneLevels.lua` (generated by Polestar from pfQuest's data, MIT;
   since 0.4.0 the rule reads a zone's danger floor, where it has one, in
   place of its lowest level).
   Polestar's `Danger.Along` and its road-through rule (journey planner) are
   not copied.
   Copyright (c) 2026 The Polestar authors. The Polestar addon is
   **GPL-3.0-only**, so `Danger.lua` is GPL-3.0-only, and `WLHelper_Nav` as a
   whole is distributed under the GNU GPL version 3.

## Upstream updates

- **2026-11-16, Polestar Nav 0.4.0** (maps of 2026-11-14): every map and
  engine file taken again from the installed Polestar Nav with the same
  renames and notice line (`PACK.json` re-pinned, its `copy` entry names the
  version). Router changes it brings: a start on indoor ground buried just
  under a city street starts from the street (Stormwind's Dwarven District,
  Polestar #1890); a path's first point gets a height when the start had
  none; `Path.ZoneTile`; spawn heights (a goal without a height next to a
  spawn where floors overlap ends on its floor), read only from maps that
  carry the field (these maps do not yet). New maps: Leffe's joined ways
  between floors (about 9,500 more edges in the Eastern Kingdoms, 18,000 in
  Kalimdor), one more town hub per continent and Zephras Isle's Shen'dar
  Village. `CREDITS.md` is 0.4.0's, unchanged.
  `Danger.lua`: Alterac Mountains' danger floor (30). `API.lua`'s Polestar
  part and `LICENSE` did not change.
